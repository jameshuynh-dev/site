Cosmo Mart

RAG-powered AI customer service

A storefront with Gleb, an AI customer service agent. Instead of letting the model guess, Gleb looks up the store's own product specs and policies (embedded with OpenAI and ranked by cosine similarity) and answers from what it finds, with responses streamed in real time.

  • Next.js 15
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind
  • Vercel AI SDK
  • OpenAI API
  • RAG
