Mimi Bot

  • Python

The task

The client wanted their own custom, private Discord bot to fulfill their needs. The goal was to create a bot that could announce Twitch streams and have certain features.

What the Discord bot does

  • Uses the Discord and Twitch APIs to send alerts to a chosen channel
  • When a stream ends, the embed updates to reflect the stream's end and how long it lasted
  • Each member gets their own custom colors and messages
  • Custom commands let the client control the bot's actions
